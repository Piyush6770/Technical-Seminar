## Executive Summary & Abstract
Implements self-distilling early exit branches with adaptive inference thresholds in transformer models, achieving a 2x-12x speedup while retaining 98% accuracy.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Demonstrates the efficacy of threshold-controlled intermediate classification heads for latency reduction.
