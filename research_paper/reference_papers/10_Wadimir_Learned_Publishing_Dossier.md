## Executive Summary & Abstract
Discusses systemic institutional pressures ('publish or perish') driving researchers to predatory journals and outlines technical verification tools needed to safeguard early-career researchers.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Provides institutional context for cost-efficient tools suitable for university libraries.
