- **Category**: Cost-Aware Acquisition & Early-Exiting Architectures

## Executive Summary & Abstract
Presents an algorithm for building a tree of classifiers where test-time feature acquisition costs are budgeted, routing easy samples through cheap feature branches while escalating hard samples.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Provides the theoretical foundation for our hierarchical stage escalation controller.
