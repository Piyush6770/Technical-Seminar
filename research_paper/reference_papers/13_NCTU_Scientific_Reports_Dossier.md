## Executive Summary & Abstract
Develops a monolithic tabular machine learning classifier combining textual, indexing, and editorial indicators for predatory journal classification, evaluated on contemporaneous cross-sectional splits.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Primary experimental baseline (monolithic all-feature acquisition) against which our cost-aware progressive architecture is benchmarked.
