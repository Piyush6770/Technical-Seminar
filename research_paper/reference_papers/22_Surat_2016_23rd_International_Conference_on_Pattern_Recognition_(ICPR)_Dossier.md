## Executive Summary & Abstract
Introduces early-exit branch architectures into deep neural networks, enabling confident predictions to terminate execution at intermediate layers and dramatically reducing compute latency.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Theoretical basis for our progressive neural heads (phi_1 through phi_4) with early exit stopping criteria.
