## Executive Summary & Abstract
Introduces a reinforcement learning and sequential cost-aware feature acquisition framework for clinical diagnosis, optimizing the tradeoff between test acquisition expense and diagnostic accuracy.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Foundational inspiration for formulating predatory journal verification as an ordered cost-constrained progressive acquisition problem.
