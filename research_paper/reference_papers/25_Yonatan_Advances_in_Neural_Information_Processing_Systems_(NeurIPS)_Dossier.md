## Executive Summary & Abstract
Defines coverage-risk performance guarantees for selective neural classifiers, introducing practical algorithms that maximize prediction coverage under a user-specified risk tolerance constraint.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Provides the mathematical basis for Figure 7's risk-coverage tradeoff and our zero-false-positive selective triage operating mode.
