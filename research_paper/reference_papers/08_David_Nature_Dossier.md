## Executive Summary & Abstract
Calls on funders, research institutions, and regulators to actively police and defund predatory publications that pollute clinical trial registries and biomedical literature with unvetted data.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Establishes institutional motivation and societal impact for high-precision automated triage systems.
