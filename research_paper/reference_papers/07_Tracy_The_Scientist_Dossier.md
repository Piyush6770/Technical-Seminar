## Executive Summary & Abstract
Investigates industrial-scale paper mills fabricating counterfeit biomedical research papers, selling authorship positions, and flooding predatory and hijacked journals with synthetic content.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Provides context on modern adversarial evasion tactics where synthetic submission velocity masks traditional predatory signals.
