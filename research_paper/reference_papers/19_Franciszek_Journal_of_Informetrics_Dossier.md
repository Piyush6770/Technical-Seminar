## Executive Summary & Abstract
Examines the emergence of generative AI and LLM tools employed by predatory publishers to automatically synthesize editorial guidelines, generate fake author biographies, and fabricate scientific peer reviews.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Directly motivates our longitudinal temporal evaluation (2014-2024) revealing feature drift under generative AI adversarial shifts.
