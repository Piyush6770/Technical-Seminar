- **Category**: Foundational Predatory Publishing Studies

## Executive Summary & Abstract
An investigative sting operation submitting 304 versions of a spoofed, scientifically flawed research paper to open-access journals. Over half accepted the counterfeit paper without substantive peer review, exposing widespread lack of quality control.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Highlights the vulnerability of purely manual submissions and reinforces the requirement for automated provenance auditing.
