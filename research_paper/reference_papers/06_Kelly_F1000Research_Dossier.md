## Executive Summary & Abstract
Systematically reviews 344 publications defining predatory journals, extracting 93 distinct checklist characteristics across operational deception, non-standard peer review, website cloaking, and false editorial board members.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Forms the theoretical and operational taxonomy for our Group A and Group B feature engineering hierarchy.
