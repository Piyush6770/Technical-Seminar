## Executive Summary & Abstract
A comprehensive empirical investigation of 1,800 predatory journals tracking 420,000 articles published between 2010 and 2014. Documents rapid publication velocity, low mean article processing charges ($178 USD), and extreme geographic concentration of authorship and publishers in developing research systems.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Empirical empirical ground-truth establishing market expansion rates and rapid publication velocity distributions used in our dataset sampling methodology.
