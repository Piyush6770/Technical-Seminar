# [4] Open access versus subscription journals: a comparison of scientific impact

- **Authors**: Bo-Christer Björk and David Solomon
- **Publication Venue**: BMC Medicine, vol. 10, no. 1, p. 73, 2012
- **Category**: Foundational Predatory Publishing Studies
- **Digital Object Identifier (DOI)**: [10.1186/1741-7015-10-73](https://doi.org/10.1186/1741-7015-10-73)
- **Full-Text Status**: 🔒 **Publisher Access**: [Direct Link](https://doi.org/10.1186/1741-7015-10-73) | [Open Access Mirror](https://bmcmedicine.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1741-7015-10-73)

---

## Executive Summary & Abstract
Analyzes citation citation frequency and citation distributions across 610 open access and subscription journals, demonstrating that legitimate open-access venues indexed in major citation registries achieve parity with traditional subscription titles.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Motivates the necessity of authoritative indexation checks (Stage 4) to distinguish genuine high-impact open access venues from deceptive mimic journals.
