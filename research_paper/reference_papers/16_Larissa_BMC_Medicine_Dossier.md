## Executive Summary & Abstract
A cross-sectional empirical comparison of 93 potential predatory biomedical journals against 99 legitimate open access titles. Identifies 13 salient discriminating characteristics, including APC disclosure clarity, rapid review promises, and contact information veracity.

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Serves as the empirical foundation for our Group B static HTML policy disclosure feature extractor.
