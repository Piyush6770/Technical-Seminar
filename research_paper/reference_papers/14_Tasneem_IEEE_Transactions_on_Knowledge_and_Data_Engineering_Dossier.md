## Executive Summary & Abstract
Applies structural tree kernel algorithms to client-side DOM representations of scholarly journal websites, demonstrating that fraudulent entities mimic visual presentation while exhibiting distorted underlying DOM tag hierarchies.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Inspiration for our Stage 3 headless DOM structural delta and obfuscated submission form features.
