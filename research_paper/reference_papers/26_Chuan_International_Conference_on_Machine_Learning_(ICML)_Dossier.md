## Executive Summary & Abstract
Discovers that modern deep neural networks with batch normalization and weight decay emit miscalibrated overconfident probabilities. Validates post-hoc Temperature Scaling as the most effective calibration method.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Underpins our temperature scaling calibration layer (T* = 1.420), reducing Expected Calibration Error (ECE) from 0.178 to 0.038.
