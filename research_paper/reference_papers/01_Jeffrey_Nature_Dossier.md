## Executive Summary & Abstract
Jeffrey Beall calls for international efforts to tackle unethical open-access publishers that prioritize profit over scientific rigor. The commentary highlights how deceptive publishers exploit the author-pays article processing charge (APC) model by establishing counterfeit journals, bypassing genuine peer review, and aggressively soliciting manuscript submissions from researchers.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Provides the foundational historical baseline and problem statement for deceptive and exploitative open-access publishing models.
