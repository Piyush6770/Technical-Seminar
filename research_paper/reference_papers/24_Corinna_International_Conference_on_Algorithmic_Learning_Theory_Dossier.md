# [24] Learning with rejection

- **Authors**: Corinna Cortes, Giulia DeSalvo, and Mehryar Mohri
- **Publication Venue**: International Conference on Algorithmic Learning Theory, Springer, pp. 67–82, 2016
- **Category**: Selective Classification, Calibration & Standards

---

## Executive Summary & Abstract
Develops a formal learning theoretic framework where classifiers are permitted to withhold predictions at a fixed cost, bounding general excess risk under non-convex rejection surrogate losses.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Theoretical foundation for our Tri-State selective abstention policy (routing ambiguous serials to human audit).
