## Executive Summary & Abstract
Introduces the strictly proper scoring rule known as the Brier Score (mean squared error of probabilistic predictions), which measures both calibration and refinement.

---

## Direct Relevance to our Research Manuscript
Serves as our primary calibration evaluation metric alongside Expected Calibration Error (ECE) in Table IV.
